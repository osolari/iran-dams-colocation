# iran-dams-colocation

Omid S. Solari, Ph.D.

Statistical assessment of the claims in *The New York Times* (16 November 2026, "War May Answer Longtime Riddle of Iranian Dams Built for No Clear Reason", Mahoozi and Solomon) that Iran's dams overlap unusually with its missile sites and may have been built to supply them.

## Main results

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Are launch bases closer to dams than random locations in Iran? | Median 48.1 km against 62.3 km expected; not significant (p = 0.146). The mean is significant (p = 0.011). |
| Are they closer than sites matched on terrain, towns and rivers? | No. The bases lie farther from dams than matched sites (p = 0.908). |
| Do towns and airports show the same apparent overlap? | Yes. Under the unadjusted test towns are much closer to dams (p < 0.001); under the matched test neither is unusual. |
| Are bases struck in June 2025 closer to dams than those not attacked? | No (48.1 against 56.9 km, p = 0.30). |
| How do Iran's launch bases rank globally for proximity to water (dam or coast)? | 50th of 55; military sites in 91% of countries lie closer to water. Relative to each country's own geography: 28th of 55 for dams, 47th for dams or coast. |
| Is Iran's dam construction unusual? | No. Its timing matches Turkey's (peak 2015 to 2019), and its density of 3.3 per 10,000 km² is below Spain (25.1), Turkey (11.9) and Morocco (6.7). |
| Do the authors advocate military action? | None of the 36 Iran-related articles catalogued does; 10 criticise the Iranian government. The only bias allegations found against Solomon (White House, NewsBusters) accuse her of being too favourable to Iran. |

Every number is generated by the code. Methods, caveats and sources are in `report/main.pdf`.

## Layout

```
data/raw/         inputs as downloaded (provenance in docs/sources.md)
data/processed/   derived datasets (GDW slim copy, Iran grid with covariates, Wikidata bases with distances)
data/external/    full GDW shapefile (not committed; see docs/sources.md)
src/              analysis code
results/tables/   CSV/JSON outputs
report/           LaTeX report, figures, auto-generated macros and tables
docs/             sources, author catalogue, GDW technical documentation
```

## Reproduce

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
bash src/fetch_data.sh        # optional: refresh public inputs (GDW must be fetched manually)
./run_all.sh                  # prepare -> analyses -> macros/tables -> PDF
```

Random seed is fixed (`SEED = 20261116` in `src/common.py`). Full run: about 2 minutes.

## Scripts

| Script | Does |
|---|---|
| `src/prepare.py` | Builds processed GDW tables, Iran 10′ grid with elevation, relief, distance to town, river, coast |
| `src/iran_analysis.py` | Nearest-dam distances for 25 bases; CSR, matched (M1 to M3) and river-constrained nulls; placebo sets; sensitivity grid; Figures 1 to 4 |
| `src/crosscountry.py` | Dam density from national counts; GDW construction timelines; Figures 5 and 6 |
| `src/global_bases.py` | Wikidata military bases worldwide; distance to dam or coast; country ranking with bootstrap CIs and own-geography ratio; Figures 7 and 8 |
| `src/make_tex.py` | Writes `report/numbers.tex` and table fragments from results |

## Limitations

The NYT text, Shokri's 533-dam map and the NYT strike map are not public; GDW (183 Iranian dams) and the Alma base list (25 sites) are the substitutes. Wikidata and GDW coverage vary by country; the own-geography ratio is the fairer cross-country measure. See Section 8 of the report.
